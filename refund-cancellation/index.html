Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: February 2025

At Nauti Amigo, we strive to provide a smooth booking experience. This policy outlines how cancellations and refunds are handled for our luxury cruise bookings. Please read it carefully before making a reservation.

1. Cancellation by You

  • More than 48 hours before the cruise: You may cancel and receive a full refund, or reschedule to another available date (subject to availability).
  • Between 24 and 48 hours before the cruise: A partial refund may be applicable, or you may reschedule. Please contact us to discuss options.
  • Less than 24 hours before the cruise: No refund will be provided. Rescheduling may be considered on a case-by-case basis at our discretion.

To cancel or request a reschedule, please contact us as soon as possible via phone 9324105081 or email sales@nautiamigo.com.

2. Refund Process

Where a refund is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed to the same payment method used for the booking.
  • Processing may take 7–14 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • You will receive a confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

3. Cancellation by Nauti Amigo

We may cancel a cruise due to unforeseen circumstances such as adverse weather, safety concerns, technical issues, or insufficient bookings. In such cases:

  • You will be notified at the earliest opportunity.
  • You will be offered a full refund or the option to reschedule to another date. We will not be liable for any additional costs (e.g., travel or accommodation) beyond the cruise booking amount.

4. No-Show

If you do not show up for your scheduled cruise without prior notice, no refund or reschedule will be provided.

5. Special Events and Promotions

For special events (e.g., New Year's Eve or other themed cruises), different cancellation and refund terms may apply. These will be clearly stated at the time of booking for such events.
