Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Nauti Amigo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website nautiamigo.com or use our cruise booking and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Booking details (dates, number of guests, package preferences)
  • Payment and billing information when you make a reservation
  • Communications when you contact us via email, phone, or WhatsApp

We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and manage your cruise bookings and payments
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and updates
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent) about offers and events
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyse site traffic, and personalise content. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Payment processors and booking partners necessary to complete your transaction
  • Service providers who assist in operating our website and business (e.g., hosting, analytics)
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies when required by law

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable. To exercise these rights or for any privacy-related queries, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
